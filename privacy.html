Privacy policy

Clear information about the data used by this website.

Information we collect

This website does not currently ask you to submit any personal information. It uses no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, embedded social media feeds or third-party font services. Its photographs and fonts are served with the website itself.

Hosting

Like any website, our hosting provider processes limited technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, so that pages can be delivered securely. This information is not used by BFK to identify you.

Future updates

If we introduce an enquiry form or mailing list, this policy will be updated before it goes live to explain who controls your information, the lawful basis for using it, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights under UK data protection law.
