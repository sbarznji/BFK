Our work

Dialogue, exchange and engagement — three ways to deepen the relationship between the United Kingdom and Kurdistan.

Diplomatic and policy dialogue

Hosting roundtables, briefings, and strategic discussions on the key issues shaping the relationship between the UK and Kurdistan.

Informed conversation begins with the opportunity to hear different perspectives. BFK provides a setting in which parliamentarians, policy specialists and other participants can discuss the issues shaping relations between the United Kingdom and Kurdistan.

Our programme is intended to include focused roundtables, parliamentary briefings and delegations. Each activity will bring the relevant people together around a clear subject, with space for questions and constructive exchange.

  • Roundtables
  • Parliamentary briefings
  • Delegations

Culture, education and exchange

Deepening mutual understanding by connecting artists, academics, and students from both nations.

Cultural and educational connections give people a fuller understanding of one another. BFK brings artists, academics, students and institutions into conversation about the experiences and ideas that connect the United Kingdom and Kurdistan.

Our programme is intended to include cultural events, academic discussions and introductions between educational institutions. We welcome proposals for collaborations that support exchange and sustained contact.

  • Cultural programmes
  • Academic exchange
  • Institutional connections

Trade and investment engagement

Unlocking new economic opportunities and building robust commercial ties between British and Kurdish markets.

Commercial relationships depend on reliable introductions and an understanding of local contexts. BFK provides opportunities for British and Kurdish business leaders to meet and discuss shared interests.

Our programme is intended to include business discussions, trade introductions and delegations. BFK’s role is to facilitate connections; participants remain responsible for their own commercial decisions and independent due diligence.

  • Trade introductions
  • Business discussions
  • Commercial delegations

A conversation starts with a connection.

For parliamentary enquiries, commercial engagement or cultural exchange, we welcome your interest in BFK.
